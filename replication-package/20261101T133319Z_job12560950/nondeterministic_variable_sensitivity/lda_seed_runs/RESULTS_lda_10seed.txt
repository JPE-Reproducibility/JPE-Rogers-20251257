Coherence scores are logged to four decimals, so a displayed gap of exactly
0.0010 can still fall inside the 1e-3 tolerance in the unrounded values. That
is what happens in seed 42's 2to1 sweep, where the run selected 15 over an
apparent arg-max of 20 at 0.5030: the deposited seed sits on the tie-break
boundary in BOTH samples, not just the 3to2 one.

The deposited models carry 15 topics in each sample (30 effects); their
expElogbeta arrays are 1869x15 and 7099x15 float32, pinned by hash in
certification/EXPECTED_FROZEN_INPUT_HASHES_REAL.csv.
